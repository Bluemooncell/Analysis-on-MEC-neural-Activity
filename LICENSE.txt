**Attribution and Disclaimer Notice**

This repository provides a collection of scripts used in the analysis of calcium imaging and behavioral data from neuroscience experiments. Many of the core analysis procedures, including calcium signal preprocessing and behavioral tracking, are adapted or built upon previous open-source projects and published methods, as listed in `README.txt`.

Specifically, portions of the code are derived from:
- Zong et al., *Cell*, 2022  
- Friedrich & Paninski, *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems*, 2016  
- Pnevmatikakis et al., *Neuron*, 2016  


We do not claim original authorship of those foundational tools. Our contributions are limited to adapting, combining, or slightly modifying parts of the original scripts to support specific experimental pipelines and visualization tasks.

Please refer to the `README.txt` file for detailed attribution and usage notes. Users are encouraged to cite the original works accordingly and to comply with any third-party licenses where applicable.
